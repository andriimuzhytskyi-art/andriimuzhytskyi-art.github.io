About me

Where the idea comes first, and form becomes language.

I am Andrii Muzhytskyi, a painter, digital craftsman, and UX/UI designer working at the intersection of art, concept, and visual structure. I create interfaces, visual systems, illustrations, and independent art projects.

In painting and digital art, I am driven by meaning, state, and the internal logic of the image. I work with minimalism, symbolism, surrealism, and realism. For me, it is essential that a work not only appears aesthetic, but also provokes reflection and creates an inner response.

As a designer, I work with interfaces where form, as a visual language, is subordinated to clarity and usability. I am drawn to clean, restrained solutions in which there are no accidental elements or visual noise.

I see art and design as a single space of thinking — where the idea is primary, and visual form becomes its precise and honest expression.
