Andrii Muzhytskyi Art

I am Andrii Muzhytskyi, a painter working in oil and digital media, where art, concept, and visual structure meet.

I work with minimalism, symbolism, and surrealism: complex ideas in simple forms, with no visual noise, where the idea always comes first.
